# Activity 5: AI Tools and Prompt Engineering

## About This Activity

This activity explored the use of artificial intelligence tools and the importance of writing effective prompts to guide AI-generated responses.

## What Have I Learned?

- I learned that clear prompts provide context, define the task, and specify the expected format, which can make AI outputs more useful.
- A challenge was getting responses that matched the task's requirements instead of relying on broad or unclear instructions.
- I improved the prompts by adding relevant context, clearer directions, and specific expectations, then reviewing the generated results.
- I can apply prompt-writing skills to brainstorming, research support, and productivity, while remembering to verify information and use AI responsibly.
